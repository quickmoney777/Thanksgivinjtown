# Facts to check before game night

Six questions are written but switched off, because they involve recent
seasons, records or stats that I can't be certain are still right. They are
in `content/questions/buckeyes-keystone.json`, each marked `"verify": true`.

To use one: check it, fix it if needed, then delete its `"verify": true` line.
To drop one: delete the whole question. Nothing marked `verify` is ever shown
in a game, and the host remote tells you how many are still waiting.

## Worth a second look anyway

These are in play. I'm confident in them, but they're the ones a sharp fan
is most likely to challenge, so read them once:

- **Woody Hayes and "That team up north."** Some fans say "that school up
  north." Both are in common use.
- **Archie Griffin is the only two-time Heisman winner.** True as of my
  information. Would change only if a past winner wins again.
- **"Hang On Sloopy" is Ohio's official rock song.** Adopted in 1985.
- **The Inclined Plane is "the world's steepest vehicular inclined plane."**
  That is how Johnstown bills it.
- **Bluff round: the Big Mac came from Uniontown (1967), the banana split
  from Latrobe (1904).** Wilmington, Ohio also claims the banana split. If
  anyone from Ohio objects, they have a case.
- **Bollywood: the film DDLJ has played in one Mumbai theater for "more than
  25 years."** It opened in 1995 and the run has had brief interruptions.
